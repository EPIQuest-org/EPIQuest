# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want <PRODUCT_NAME> to be a project where everyone can contribute, learn and give feedback without fear of harassment or disrespect. This applies to every team member, reviewer, tester and visitor, whatever their experience, background, identity or abilities.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and kind. Assume good intentions, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person. Reviews are constructive and specific.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and answer review comments.
- Share knowledge. Nobody is expected to know everything.
- Respect other people's time, workload and availability.
- Credit other people's work, including content and tools that helped us.
- Keep private information private, including information shared in private messages.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Personal attacks, mockery of someone's skills, or deliberately blocking someone's work.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Taking credit for someone else's work.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every space of the project: the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, comments), Discord, shared documents, meetings and presentations, and any situation where someone represents the project.
